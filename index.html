Small tip

Use the Pan tool or right-click and drag to move around the infinite canvas. Hold Shift while drawing to make straight lines, squares, and circles. Select assets with a rectangle or lasso, and Shift-click to add or remove assets from a selection. Select an object to resize it using the handles. Glitter is a rainbow pen; the laser pointer fades away and is not saved. Boards autosave locally; public rooms need no password, private rooms ask for one.
